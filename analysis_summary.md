# Analysis Summary

## Objective
The objective of this project is to understand Superstore business performance using sales and profit data.

## Key Analysis Areas
1. Sales performance
2. Profit performance
3. Category performance
4. Regional performance
5. Customer segment performance
6. Product performance
7. Monthly trends

## Insight Template
After connecting the actual dataset, record findings such as:

- Highest sales category: __________
- Highest profit category: __________
- Best-performing region: __________
- Most profitable product: __________
- Best customer segment: __________
- Highest-sales month: __________

## Conclusion
The dashboard helps identify sales trends, profitable categories, strong regions, and products that may need attention. These insights can support better business decisions.
